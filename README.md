# PCC Foundation

The PCC Foundation consists of three foundational papers establishing Personal Cognitive Computing as an emerging computing paradigm, Personal Cognitive Engineering as its engineering discipline and Personal Cognitive Infrastructure as its research programme.

Together, the papers provide the conceptual, engineering and research foundations for computing environments through which individuals can benefit from computational reasoning while retaining meaningful authority over their information, memory, evidence, instructions and accumulated knowledge.

## The broader positioning

1. **Personal Cognitive Sovereignty (PCS)**  
   The overarching human mission: people retain meaningful authority over their information, memory, knowledge, evidence, instructions and computational reasoning.

2. **Personal Cognitive Computing (PCC)**  
   The emerging computing paradigm and field: the next chapter in computing, in which computing becomes personal again as it becomes cognitive.

3. **Personal Cognitive Engineering (PCE)**  
   The applied engineering discipline and methodology through which PCC is translated into architectures, systems, shared capabilities and reference implementations.

4. **Personal Cognitive Infrastructure (PCI)**  
   The infrastructural and scientific research programme investigating the unresolved engineering problems and shared foundations required to make PCC possible.

5. **Nivel**  
   A concrete reference implementation of PCC, developed according to PCE principles and contributing practical evidence to the PCI research programme.

6. **Institute for Personal Cognitive Computing**  
   The proposed future independent institutional home for the research discipline, publications, shared frameworks, evaluation and public-interest development.

In short:

> **Personal Cognitive Sovereignty is the mission.**  
> **PCC defines the paradigm.**  
> **PCE defines the engineering discipline.**  
> **PCI defines the infrastructure-oriented research programme.**  
> **Nivel provides a reference implementation.**  
> **The Institute provides the institutional home.**
